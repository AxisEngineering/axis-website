Define the launch

A linear induction motor accelerates the vehicle across an air gap, without relying on wheel grip. Specify entry speed, target exit speed, launch length and the allowed acceleration profile together.

The duty may also include low-speed station moves, steady dark-ride sections and the hand-off into coasting. These operating points belong in the specification, not just the launch peak.

What sets the motor rating?

  • Load and trajectory: vehicle mass, speed, acceleration, gradients and launch distance.
  • Repetition: firing rate and recovery time determine whether motor and plate heat accumulates.
  • Electrical demand: a short launch can require high peak current and power even when average demand is modest. Match the drive, site supply and any energy storage to the same duty.
  • Installation: compare single- and double-sided layouts, running gap and available space.
